# Sales Funnel Optimization & Revenue Leakage Analysis (SQL & Python) 
## Business Problem 
An e-commerce business is generating strong lead volume but struggling 
to convert those leads into revenue. 
Management suspects: - Significant drop-offs across the sales funnel - Excessive discounting reducing margins - Delays in sales follow-ups hurting conversions 
## Objective 
The goal of this project is to: - Measure conversion rates across funnel stages - Identify where customers drop off - Quantify revenue leakage due to discounts and delays - Provide actionable, data-driven recommendations 
## Dataset 
This project uses a realistic, simulated business dataset structured 
like a real production system. 
Tables: - Customers: signup date, acquisition channel, country - Orders: list price, discount, final price, deal status - Sales Events: lead, demo, proposal, closed stages 
Scale: 
- 10,000 customers - 50,000 orders - 200,000 sales events 
## Key Analyses 
### 1. Funnel Analysis - Funnel stages: Lead → Demo → Proposal → Closed - Conversion rates between each stage - Drop-off percentages at every step 
### 2. Revenue Leakage Analysis - Expected price vs actual price - Revenue lost due to discounting - Leakage by sales rep, channel, and deal size 
### 3. Time-to-Conversion - Delay between sales events - Impact of delays on deal success - Identification of high-risk stalled deals 
## Tools & Techniques - SQL: CTEs, window functions, joins, aggregations - Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib - Jupyter Notebook for analysis & storytelling 
## Key Insights - Over 30% of total revenue leakage came from discounts above 20% - Deals delayed more than 14 days had significantly lower close rates - Some acquisition channels had high lead volume but poor conversion 
quality 
## Business Recommendations - Introduce discount approval thresholds - Enforce follow-up SLAs within 7 days - Reallocate budget toward high-converting channels - Prioritize stalled deals with high revenue potential
